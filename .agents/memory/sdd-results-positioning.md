---
name: SDD results positioning
description: Durable messaging direction for explaining the agency's commercial value
---

Stuffed Dog Digital’s site must explain how the agency gets results, not stop at celebrating creativity or humanity.

**Why:** The user identified results as a very important part of what the agency does and wants prospects to understand the commercial value behind the work.

**How to apply:** Frame the method as a connected system: identify market and sales friction, engineer conversion-focused assets, distribute them to qualified audiences, then measure and optimize. Keep claims credible and outcome-oriented.
