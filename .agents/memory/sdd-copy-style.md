---
name: SDD copy punctuation
description: Punctuation constraint for all Stuffed Dog Digital website writing.
---

Never use em dashes anywhere in Stuffed Dog Digital site copy, metadata, or labels.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a site-wide ban on em dashes.

**How to apply:** Rewrite with periods, commas, colons, semicolons, or natural sentence restructuring instead of substituting another dash character.
